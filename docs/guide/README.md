# Fairfax user guide

A guide for people who have just installed Fairfax. Read the first two chapters and you
can use it. The rest is there when you want it.

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md): install, set up Omarchy, the first launch and the three panels.
2. [The basics](02-basics.md): lists, adding and editing tasks, Quick Entry, dates, stars, and the focus lists.
3. [Organizing your tasks](03-organizing.md): projects, checklists, sections, tags, places, archiving, and more windows.
4. [Search and Smart Lists](04-search-and-smart-lists.md): finding things, the search syntax, the rule builder and saved searches.
5. [My Day](05-my-day.md): a daily plan built from your dates, and the end-of-day review.
6. [Repeats and alerts](06-repeats-and-alerts.md): repeating tasks, alerts, snooze and notifications.
7. [The keyboard](07-keyboard.md): how the keys work, every shortcut, and changing them.
8. [Sync and backups](08-sync-and-backups.md): git, folder, WebDAV, CalDAV and Todoist, plus backups and restore.
9. [Mail to tasks](09-mail.md): turning email into tasks from a mailbox you own.
10. [Moving in](10-moving-in.md): importing from other apps and exporting your tasks.
11. [Scripts and agents](11-scripts-and-agents.md): the terminal, JSON commands, links and suggested changes.
12. [Settings](12-settings.md): every setting, pane by pane.
13. [Accessibility](13-accessibility.md): screen readers, keyboard-only use and text size.

[`shots.txt`](shots.txt) lists the screenshots the guide uses and how to regenerate each
one from the built-in sample data.
