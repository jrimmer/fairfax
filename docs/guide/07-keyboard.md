# The keyboard

Fairfax is built so you can do nearly everything without lifting your hands from
the keyboard. You never *have* to. Every action has a mouse way too. But once a few
keys are in your fingers, a whole list of tasks takes seconds.

## How the keyboard works

There are two kinds of shortcut.

1. **Bare keys** (a single letter or digit) work while the **task list has focus**.
   Select a task with the arrow keys, press **t**, and it is due today. These keys
   do nothing while you are typing in a field, so they never get in your way.
2. **Keys with Ctrl or Alt** work anywhere in the window, including while you edit.

A few habits worth building:

- **↑ ↓** move the selection. Hold **Shift** to select several. **Ctrl+A** selects
  all.
- **Return** edits the selected task. **Esc** finishes editing, or clears the
  selection.
- **Space** opens Quick Look.
- Most commands that need a choice (**d**, **k**, **m**, **l**) open a small box where
  you type, then press **Return**.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes anything. **Ctrl+Shift+Z** redoes it.
- Press **?** to see every shortcut, as you have it set, right in the app.

Fairfax's **Ctrl** is what Macs call ⌘, because the Super key belongs to your
window manager. On macOS the overlay shows ⌘ and ⌥; this guide always writes Ctrl
and Alt.

## Change a shortcut

Open **Settings → Shortcuts** (**Ctrl+,**, then choose *Shortcuts*).

1. Find the command. Type in the filter box to search by name or by key.
2. Click its key button. It says it is listening.
3. Press the key combination you want. Press **Esc** to cancel.

Fairfax checks your choice:

- Keys used for moving the selection, **Return**, **Esc**, **Tab**, **Ctrl+A**,
  **Ctrl+C**, **Ctrl+X**, **Ctrl+V**, **Ctrl+Return** and the hold-**Alt** reveal are
  reserved. They cannot be changed, because the list and the editor depend on them.
- A shortcut that works anywhere in the window needs **Ctrl** or **Alt**, or a function
  key (**F1** to **F35**). Bare letters are only for the task list.
- If the key already belongs to another command, Fairfax tells you and lets you
  *swap* the two, or take the key and leave the other command with none.

**Reset All…** puts every shortcut back to its default after you confirm.

The **?** overlay, the editor's shortcut menu and the tray always show your current
keys, so you never need to remember the changes.

## Quick Entry outside the window

The key that opens Quick Entry from anywhere is not one of these. It is a binding in
your desktop (Hyprland on Omarchy) that runs `fairfax --quick`. On Omarchy,
`fairfax setup omarchy` adds it. See [Getting started](01-getting-started.md).
